# Changelog

## 1.1.0

### New
- **World chests.** Battered, Solid, Iron Bound, Mithril Bound, footlockers, coffers and more, out in the world and in dungeons. While you cast "Opening", the chest rattles harder as the bar fills; when the cast finishes, the cards are dealt with what's really inside.
- **Group loot for chests.** Items that go to a roll or the master looter get a card too, stamped *UP FOR ROLL* or *LOOTER'S CALL*, and the card updates when someone wins it (or everyone passes). The addon only takes what's yours.
- **Auto-collect** (Settings, on by default): the loot is taken a moment after the last card, no COLLECT click. With batch mode, OPEN ANOTHER is up right after.
- **Drop chances under every item** in the "What's inside" panel, no hover needed.
- **Keep the odds up during the show** (Settings, off by default): the "What's inside" panel stays on screen while the cards and reel play.
- **Empty boxes get a show too.** A clam with nothing in it spins onto dust ("Not even the meat."), and a card box that gives nothing deals all dust. The Demo room has an **Empty** button for boxes that can come up empty.

### Changed
- World chest rarities come from Auction House prices, ranked within each chest, so a green can be a big deal in a low-level chest.

## 1.0.0

- First release: card pulls and reels for lockboxes, junkboxes, fishing trunks, clams, crates and mage Comprehension; rarity tiers, the hunting list, Elusive finds, Your Luck, batch mode, My boxes and the Demo room.
